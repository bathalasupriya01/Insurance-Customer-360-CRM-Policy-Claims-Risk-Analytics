# Key Findings

1. **Marketing response is low overall:** 14.32% of 9,134 customers responded.
2. **Agent is the strongest observed channel:** 19.15% response, versus 11–12% across the other channels.
3. **Offer2 stands out:** 23.38% observed response. Offer2 + Agent reaches 31.90% across 978 customers.
4. **High-value opportunity:** 1,936 customers are in the top CLV quartile and did not respond.
5. **Premium coverage combines value and claim severity:** average CLV is $10,895.60 and average claim is $651.39.
6. **The top 10% claim threshold is about $773.43:** customers above it average $1,066.42 in observed claims.
7. **Luxury vehicle segments are claim-heavy:** average claims exceed $1,000 for Luxury Car and Luxury SUV, but segment sizes are small and should be shown with counts.
8. **Open complaints are material:** 20.6% of customers have at least one open complaint.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Do not describe marketing response as renewal or churn.
- Do not claim Offer2 or Agent *caused* higher response.
- Do not describe claim bands as fraud.
- Do not use the claim-to-premium proxy as a real insurance loss ratio.
- Do not use this sample for live pricing or underwriting.
