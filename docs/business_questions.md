# Business Questions

## CRM
1. What is the overall marketing response rate?
2. Which sales channels are associated with stronger response?
3. Which renewal-offer types are associated with stronger response?
4. Which offer/channel combinations deserve controlled follow-up testing?
5. Which high-value customers did not respond and may warrant a different CRM treatment?

## Customer value
6. How does marketing response differ across CLV tiers?
7. Which customer tiers have the highest claims and premiums?
8. Are there high-value single-policy customers who could be reviewed for cross-sell suitability?

## Insurance / policy
9. Which policy types dominate the portfolio?
10. How do CLV, premiums and claims differ by coverage?
11. Which vehicle classes have higher observed claims?
12. Which vehicle/coverage combinations deserve deeper review?

## Claims risk monitoring
13. What do the claim distribution and upper-tail thresholds look like?
14. Which customer groups sit in high and very-high observed claim bands?
15. Where are open complaints concentrated?
16. Which apparent claim differences may be driven by mix and therefore require more data?

## Governance
17. Which conclusions are descriptive versus predictive?
18. What information is missing for a real underwriting/pricing decision?
